1. Executive Summary & Problem Context

The Indian Railways (IR) operates one of the world's most congested and heavily utilized railway networks, transporting over 24 million passengers daily across 68,000+ route kilometers. Existing passenger information systems (e.g., legacy NTES and static timetable trackers) rely predominantly on single-station linear extrapolation and static speed matrices. When an unexpected operational disruption occurs—such as sectional saturation, freight precedence, caution orders, or inclement weather—these linear systems fail to model the non-linear cascade ripple effect, resulting in erratic arrival estimates, station platform gridlocks, and passenger anxiety.

🎯 The TrackPulse Mission (SIH26028)

TrackPulse delivers an enterprise-grade, real-time Dynamic ETA Forecasting Platform tailored specifically to the operational realities of Indian Railways. It integrates pre-trained Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (LightGBM), sectional quantile uncertainty bounds (P10, P50, P90), multi-train network graph propagation, and a 4-tier causal reasoning engine with live telemetry spanning all 2,810 Indian Railway trains.

2. Core Technical Approach & Mathematical Framework

TrackPulse shifts the paradigm from deterministic timestamp forecasting to probabilistic sectional quantile modeling. Rather than predicting a single brittle arrival time, the engine calculates the probability distribution of inter-station traversal run-times across intermediate railway blocks.

Tier 1: Sectional Quantile Regression P10 / P50 / P90
Evaluates physical track blocks, maximum permissible speeds (MPS), permanent speed restrictions (PSR), and scheduled slack buffers. $$\text{ETA}_{s} = \text{Dep}_{s-1} + \mathbb{E}[\Delta T_{(s-1, s)} \mid \mathbf{X}_{\text{sec}}] + \delta_{\text{dwell}}$$ Where $\mathbf{X}_{\text{sec}}$ encodes Sectional Saturation Index ($V/C$), block signalling density, and gradient characteristics.
Tier 2: LightGBM Propagation Classifier LightGBM v4.2
Predicts whether an existing delay will amplify ($\Delta D > 0$) or recover ($\Delta D < 0$) over subsequent 50km–300km horizons. Features include: historical delay delta, day of week, train priority weight (Vande Bharat / Rajdhani = 1.0, Express = 0.7, Passenger = 0.4), and junction bottleneck queues.

4-Tier Causal Reasoning Engine

Unlike black-box neural networks, TrackPulse pairs every ETA prediction with a structured, explainable causal root attribution:

Causal Category Trigger Conditions & Telemetry Signals Algorithmic Impact on ETA Uncertainty
🔴 CONGESTION Sectional saturation ratio $V/C > 0.85$, preceding train headway < 8 km, junction platform dwell queues. Expands P90 tail risk by $+18\text{--}45\text{ min}$; dampens expected make-up recovery rate by $60\%$.
🟠 PRECEDENCE Premium rake (12001/12431 Rajdhani, 20607 Vande Bharat) trailing within 25km block on double-line section. Forces lower-priority rake into intermediate loop line detention for 12–20 minutes.
🟡 WEATHER Dense Fog / Low Visibility (< 200m), convective monsoon squall, extreme heat rail buckling caution. Caps Loco Pilot Maximum Permissible Speed (MPS) at $60\text{ km/h}$ under Automatic Block territory.
🔵 TURNOVER Rake incoming buffer slack < 45 minutes at terminal turnaround station. Propagates source departure delay to downstream pairing return rake automatically.

4. End-to-End Operational Workflow

The following diagrams illustrate the synchronous and asynchronous data pipelines powering real-time predictions:

Workflow A: Real-Time Dynamic ETA Pipeline
1. User Query / Telemetry Trigger
User inputs Train Number (e.g. 12952) or station boardMAS.
2. Live Telemetry Fetch (RailRadar API)
Fetch current coordinates, last reported halt, and speed.
3. Feature Engineering & Context Vector
Construct 24-feature vector: headway, $V/C$ ratio, priority weight.
4. LightGBM & Quantile Inference
Compute $P_{10}$ (best), $P_{50}$ (expected), $P_{90}$ (worst) arrivals.
5. 4-Tier Causal Reasoner & Advisory
Tag delay cause & dispatch passenger recommendations.
Workflow B: What-If Dispatch Simulation
1. Section Injection
Section Controller injects speed restriction / track block.
2. Multi-Train Graph Propagation
Recursive BFS traversal across all active rakes in the corridor.
3. Conflict & Overtake Resolution
Evaluate loop-line siding availability for lower priority rakes.
4. Cascade Impact Calculation
Calculate cumulative network delay passenger-hours.
5. Optimal Dispatch Strategy
Recommend optimal platform reallocations & hold times.
